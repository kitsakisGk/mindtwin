\begin{abstract}
Cognitive Digital Twins (CDTs) extend digital twins with perception, memory, reasoning and
decision-making grounded in semantic knowledge. Despite growing interest, most CDT work remains
conceptual or proprietary, and the recent integration of large language models (LLMs) into twins
lacks quantitative evaluation. We present \emph{mindtwin}, an open-source CDT framework that
connects a live twin, a knowledge graph, prognostic models and an LLM agent. We further introduce
\emph{TwinQA}, a benchmark of questions with ground-truth answers over the NASA C-MAPSS turbofan

\end{abstract}
