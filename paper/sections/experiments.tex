\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experiments}

\subsection{Dataset}
\label{sec:dataset}

We use the NASA C-MAPSS turbofan engine degradation dataset~\citep{saxena2008damage}. It consists
of four subsets (Table~\ref{tab:cmapss}) of simulated run-to-failure trajectories. Each row is one
operating cycle of one engine, described by three operational settings and 21 sensor
measurements. Training engines run until failure; test engines stop before failure and are scored
on their remaining useful life (RUL) at the last observed cycle. We note that the official readme
swaps the FD004 engine counts; we use the counts found in the data files.

\begin{table}[ht]
  \centering
  \caption{C-MAPSS subsets. Life is the number of cycles to failure of training engines.}
  \label{tab:cmapss}
  \begin{tabular}{lccccc}
    \toprule
    Subset & Conditions & Fault modes & Train / test engines & Life (min / median / max) & Test RUL \\
    \midrule
    FD001 & 1 & HPC      & 100 / 100 & 128 / 199 / 362 & 7--145 \\
    FD002 & 6 & HPC      & 260 / 259 & 128 / 199 / 378 & 6--194 \\
    FD003 & 1 & HPC, fan & 100 / 100 & 145 / 220 / 525 & 6--145 \\
    FD004 & 6 & HPC, fan & 249 / 248 & 128 / 234 / 543 & 6--195 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

\paragraph{Operating conditions.} In FD002 and FD004, raw sensor values are dominated by six
operating conditions, which we identify exactly by rounding the three settings. Normalising each
sensor per condition, with statistics fitted on training data only, raises the strongest rank
correlation between a sensor and RUL in FD002 from 0.25 to 0.71
(Figure~\ref{fig:condition-norm}).

  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{cmapss_condition_normalisation_fd002}
  \caption{Static pressure at the HPC outlet (s11) in FD002 before and after per-condition
  normalisation.}

\end{figure}

\paragraph{Fault signatures.} Comparing FD001 (HPC fault only) with FD003 (HPC and fan faults)
shows that faults leave different signatures: five sensors that are strongly correlated with RUL
under the HPC fault (s7, s12, s15, s20, s21) lose this correlation when the fan fault is also
present (Figure~\ref{fig:fault-signatures}). This motivates representing which component each
sensor observes and which fault modes affect it, i.e.\ the knowledge graph of
Section~\ref{sec:architecture}.

\subsection{Evaluation protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

The protocol was fixed before training any model, following the recommendations
of~\citet{ramasso2014benchmarking}. Each subset is evaluated separately on its official test set,
scoring every test engine once at its last cycle. Hyperparameters are tuned on 20\% of the
training engines (split by engine, not by row). The training target is piecewise-linear RUL capped
at 125 cycles, with a sensitivity analysis over the cap. We report RMSE and the PHM08
score~\citep{saxena2008damage}, as mean $\pm$ standard deviation over five seeds.
